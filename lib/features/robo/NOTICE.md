# Attribution

The Robo mascot is **new Dart code**. Its look and behaviour were *inspired by*
[fayazara/robot-heads](https://github.com/fayazara/robot-heads) (React/TypeScript,
MIT, (c) 2026 Fayaz Ahmed), whose README, `faces.ts` and `sim.ts` I read.

No source was copied or translated line by line, and the rendering is different
(the original is a lit 3D mesh projected to canvas with an LED matrix; this is
2D vector shapes with gradients and parallax). A few *design values* were
informed by the original: the state names and their face concepts, antenna
light colours, and rough motion timings. Out of caution the MIT notice is
